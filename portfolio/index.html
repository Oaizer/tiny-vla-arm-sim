How to read this work

The VLA models were trained and tested with simulated scenes and simulator labels. V13 scores grasp candidates with full pick, lift, and place outcomes. The B601-RS challenge series uses its own articulated URDF and simulator-state teacher. It is separate from the Panda VLA results.

Open methods and limits

Policy

A small vision-language-action policy maps the camera and instruction to arm commands. The original feedback controller remains the default.

World model

A JEPA-style model predicts future visual and arm state from proposed commands. It uses simulator labels and is not fully self-supervised.

Training labels

Earlier versions learned from finger contact. V13 learned from full simulated task outcomes. The live controller still uses camera and finger checks.

Limit

V13 gained six successes across 80 matched trials, but the exact paired test gave p = 0.210. This is early evidence for one simulated object and task. Feedback remains the default until larger tests confirm the gain.

All results, scenes, and videos on this page come from simulation. Results remain tied to their protocols and seed ranges. New evidence should be added to the iteration record.
